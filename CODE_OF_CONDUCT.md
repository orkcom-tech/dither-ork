# Code of Conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short.

## Expected

- Be civil. Disagree with the work, not the person.
- Assume the other person read the docs and still got stuck. That is usually a
  docs problem.
- Say what you tried and what happened. "It does not work" wastes both our time.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning comments about anyone's identity — who they are, where they are
  from, what they believe, how they look, or how they speak.
- Sexual attention or imagery anywhere in the project.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Persistently derailing threads after being asked to stop.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any other space that
carries this project's name.

## Enforcement

Report to <abyssmemes@gmail.com>. Reports stay private.

The maintainer will edit, hide, or delete offending content and may block the
account. There is one maintainer and no appeals process; a single warning is
normal for a first lapse, and repeat behaviour is not warned again.
